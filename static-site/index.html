Our Story

Founder

Angel Gerena

Finesse Media LLC was born out of a simple conviction — every business deserves a professional digital presence, regardless of size or budget. I founded Finesse Media right here in Deltona, Florida, and grew it from a photography-focused studio into a full-service creative and digital agency serving small businesses, corporations, churches, and nonprofits across Central Florida and beyond.

As a bilingual Creative Director, I bridge the gap between the English and Spanish-speaking business communities — a market largely underserved by agencies that either charge too much or communicate too little. When you work with Finesse Media, you work directly with me. No outsourcing. No hand-offs. Just dedicated, high-quality creative output delivered with honesty and care.

Faith is not a tagline here — it is the foundation. Jeremiah 29:11 sits at the heart of every decision I make at Finesse Media. I believe there is a plan, and that plan includes serving others well through creativity and technology. That belief drives everything I do.
