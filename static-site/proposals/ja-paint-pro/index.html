Questions

Good to know

Are estimates really free?

Yes. We visit, measure and leave a written quote with no obligation.

Do I need to move my furniture?

No. We move and cover furniture, protect floors and put everything back when we finish.

How long does a typical job take?

Most interior rooms take one day. A full home interior is usually 3 to 5 days, exteriors 4 to 7 depending on size and weather.

What paint do you use?

Premium lines from trusted brands, matched to the surface and Florida's climate. Your quote lists the exact product.

How long before I can park on my new garage floor?

Most garages are coated in a single day. At your estimate we'll tell you exactly when you can walk on it and when you can park on it.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Proof of license and insurance is available on request before any work begins.
