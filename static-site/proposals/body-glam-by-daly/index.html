FAQ

Good to know

01Is body sculpting surgery?

No. Every body treatment I offer is non-invasive, with no surgery. Results vary from person to person, and many clients book a series for the best results.

02How should I prepare for my facial?

Come as you are. Tell me about the skin products you use, any medications, pregnancy or recent treatments so I can tailor your facial safely.

03Do you treat teens?

Yes. I offer a teen acne facial. A parent or guardian books the visit and signs the consent form for clients under 18.

04How do I pay?

By card through Vagaro when you book, or by Zelle to Body Glam by Daly LLC.

05How do I cancel or reschedule?

Use the link in your Vagaro confirmation, or call or text me. Please give 24 hours notice.
