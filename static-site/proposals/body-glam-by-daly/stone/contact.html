FAQ

Good to know

Is body sculpting surgery?

No. Every body treatment Daly offers is non-invasive, with no surgery. Results vary from person to person, and many clients book a series for the best results.

How should I prepare for a facial?

Come as you are. Tell Daly about the skin products you use, any medications, pregnancy or recent treatments so she can tailor your facial safely.

Do you treat teens?

Yes, with the teen acne facial. A parent or guardian books the visit and signs the consent form for clients under 18.

How do I pay?

By card through Vagaro when you book, or by Zelle to Body Glam by Daly LLC.

How do I cancel or reschedule?

Use the link in your Vagaro confirmation, or call or text Daly. Please give 24 hours notice.
