HOW BOOKING WORKS

How booking works

  1. Free consultation. We talk through your date, your vision, and the collection that fits.
  2. Reserve your date. A 30% retainer and a simple agreement hold your date. Retainers are paid by card or bank transfer.
  3. Your balance. Due 14 days before your event or session. Pay in full, or choose a pay-over-time option at checkout.
  4. Your photos. Sneak peeks within 48 hours. Full galleries in 2 weeks for sessions and 4 to 6 weeks for weddings and quinceañeras.

PAY OVER TIME

Pay over time

  • When you pay your balance, you can choose Klarna or Cash App Afterpay at checkout to split it into smaller payments. You will see the plan before you agree to it, and choosing a plan never costs you more with me.

Payment plans are provided by Klarna and Cash App Afterpay and are subject to eligibility and approval. Plan terms, including any interest or late fees, are set by the provider. Pay-over-time options apply to personal sessions and celebrations; business and corporate sessions are invoiced directly.

WHAT TO ASK ANY PHOTOGRAPHER

What to ask any photographer

  • Will I receive sneak peeks, and how soon?
  • How many edited images are included, and when is the full gallery delivered?
  • Is there a second photographer for large events?
  • Are hair and makeup, albums, or prints included or extra?
  • Can you communicate with my whole family in Spanish?
  • Do you offer an event website or a way to collect guests' photos?
  • What happens if I need to reschedule?
